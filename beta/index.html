WHAT YOU’LL BE TESTING

One training home instead of six disconnected tools.

01

Flexible workout plans

Choose the workout focus that fits your body and schedule instead of being locked into a rigid Monday-to-Friday order.

02

Guided workout logging

Track sets, reps, load, RPE, exercise swaps, rest, progression, and past workouts from one place.

03

Progress that stays useful

Review workout history, logged loads, check-ins, body-weight trends, and performance over time.

04

Nutrition tracking

Log calories, protein, carbs, fats, and water, with guidance tied to the goals you set in BodySmith.

05

The Forge

Post workouts, photos, GIFs, progress updates, comments, reactions, and playable YouTube links in the community feed.

06

Built for real life

Missed logging yesterday? Add a past workout. Training with a friend? Pick a different focus. The app adapts instead of scolding you.
