Barbell Bench Press
Offline modev2.4

Two-position movement guide. Read the setup cue below. Muscle highlights are illustrative.

ChestBarbellHorizontal push

Setup

Lie on a flat bench with feet planted. Grip just outside shoulder width. Use a spotter or correctly positioned safety arms.

Movement

Unrack over shoulders, lower to mid-chest with forearms vertical, then press up and slightly back.

Keep wrists stacked above elbows.

Primary: Chest · Secondary: Triceps, Anterior deltoids
Intermediate · Bilateral · weighted

Common mistakes
  • Bouncing off the chest
  • Flaring elbows excessively
